Ingredients

Carbonated water, sugar, acidity regulator (E330, E331), permitted class II preservative (E211), vitamins (B2, B3, B6, B12), taurine (0.4%), caffeine (0.03%). Contains permitted natural colour (150d) and added flavours (natural and nature-identical flavouring substances). Contains no fruits.

High caffeine content (75 mg per 250 ml). Not recommended for children, pregnant or lactating women, or persons sensitive to caffeine. Do not consume more than 500 ml per day. Store in a cool, dry, hygienic place away from direct sunlight.
