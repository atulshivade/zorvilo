Our Story

Zorvilo is a young Indian beverage brand with an old-fashioned idea: make every drink in the chiller worth reaching for.

The idea

One brand,
the whole chiller

Most beverage brands pick a lane. Zorvilo was built the other way round — as a house of drinks that can cover a retailer's cold cabinet from top shelf to bottom.

That means an energy drink with a real functional formulation, four carbonated soft drinks priced for everyday purchase, small-format fruit drinks for younger shoppers, aloe vera juices for the wellness aisle and a range of 0.0% brews for the evening occasion. Twenty-one products, one supplier relationship, one brand block on the shelf.

Quality & compliance

Made properly,
declared honestly

Every Zorvilo unit carries its licence numbers, batch code, manufacture date and expiry date. Nothing about the formulation is hidden behind marketing language.

FSSAI licensed

Brand-owned, marketed and manufactured under FSSAI licence, so the same company answers for the formulation and the fill.

Full traceability

Batch number, manufacture date and expiry date are printed on the base of every can and bottle.

Clear advisories

Caffeine content, consumption limits and storage guidance are stated plainly on pack and repeated on this site.

Recyclable packaging

Aluminium cans and PET bottles carry recycling marks and keep-your-city-clean guidance.

Trade-ready formats

160 ml, 200 ml and 250 ml singles plus the six-can party pack, sized for impulse and route sales.

Export ready

Shelf-stable formats with an eighteen-month shelf life, suited to long-haul distribution.
